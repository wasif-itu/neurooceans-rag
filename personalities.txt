Proposed Test Personas — 30 American Public Figures

All are living, publicly active, non-political, and have well-documented childhoods, education, and career histories.

Film & Television

Tom Hanks — Actor and producer; four decades of documented film work, also a writer and historian of WWII subjects.
Viola Davis — Actress; EGOT winner, trained at Juilliard, published memoir covering a childhood in poverty in Rhode Island.
Ke Huy Quan — Actor; 1980s child star who left acting for roughly twenty years before returning and winning an Oscar.
Octavia Spencer — Actress; Alabama upbringing, years of small roles before an Academy Award.
Michael B. Jordan — Actor and director; raised in Newark, went from child modeling to leading roles.
Reese Witherspoon — Actress turned media entrepreneur; founded a production company and a national book club.
Sterling K. Brown — Actor; St. Louis upbringing, Stanford undergraduate, NYU graduate training, extensive television career.
Ava DuVernay — Film director; worked as a publicist before changing careers in her thirties.

Music & Stage
9. Dolly Parton — Singer and songwriter; extensive philanthropic record in children's literacy and medical research.
10. Yo-Yo Ma — Cellist; born in Paris to Chinese parents, raised in New York, studied at Juilliard and Harvard.
11. Jon Batiste — Musician; New Orleans musical family, Juilliard training, television bandleader and Grammy winner.
12. Lin-Manuel Miranda — Composer and playwright; raised in Washington Heights, studied at Wesleyan, Broadway career.
13. Brandi Carlile — Singer-songwriter; rural Washington State upbringing, self-taught, runs her own charitable foundation.

Team Sports
14. Stephen Curry — NBA guard; son of an NBA player, lightly recruited out of high school, played at Davidson and completed his degree years later.
15. A'ja Wilson — WNBA forward; South Carolina native, diagnosed with dyslexia as a child, starred at her hometown university.
16. Sue Bird — Retired WNBA guard; Long Island upbringing, UConn, long international playing career.
17. Patrick Mahomes — NFL quarterback; son of a Major League pitcher, multi-sport athlete, played at Texas Tech.
18. Aaron Judge — MLB outfielder; adopted as an infant, small-town California upbringing, played at Fresno State.
19. Sabrina Ionescu — WNBA guard; daughter of Romanian immigrants, record-setting college career at Oregon.

Individual Sports & Racing
20. Simone Biles — Gymnast; raised by her grandparents after time in foster care, homeschooled, most decorated athlete in her sport.
21. Katie Ledecky — Swimmer; Washington DC area upbringing, competed for Stanford while completing her degree.
22. Chase Elliott — NASCAR driver; son of a Hall of Fame driver, Georgia native, began racing as a young teenager.
23. Josef Newgarden — IndyCar driver; Tennessee native, moved alone to the UK at eighteen to pursue racing.
24. Mikaela Shiffrin — Alpine skier; raised in a skiing family, trained at a Vermont academy, holds the record for World Cup wins.

Science, Space, Food & Philanthropy
25. Temple Grandin — Animal science professor and autism advocate; extensively documented childhood, author of several memoirs.
26. Christina Koch — NASA astronaut; North Carolina native, engineering degrees from NC State, holds a record for longest single spaceflight by a woman.
27. Victor Glover — NASA astronaut and Navy test pilot; California native, Cal Poly graduate with multiple advanced degrees.
28. Katalin Karikó — Biochemist; Hungarian-born naturalized American, decades of rejected funding applications before a Nobel Prize.
29. Ina Garten — Cookbook author and television host; former White House budget analyst who left government to buy a specialty food shop.
30. Marcus Samuelsson — Chef and restaurateur; born in Ethiopia, adopted and raised in Sweden, built his career in New York.
